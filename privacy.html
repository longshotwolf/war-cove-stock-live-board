Master Stock Board

What this board does

The War Cove Master Stock Board shows live stock market readings. All readings are computed in your own browser from public stock market data.

Data we collect

None. The board and its app form collect no personal data. There are no accounts on this board, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies set by this page.

Data stored on your device

If you install the board as an app, your device stores only the app's static shell files (the app icon and install manifest) so it can open with the board's look. The board page itself and all market data are always loaded fresh from the network — nothing about your activity is stored or sent anywhere.

Third parties

Your browser connects to public stock market data feeds to produce the readings. Those connections carry only the market data the board displays; we do not receive, log, or sell any information from them. No personal data is sold or shared with anyone.

Seat sign-in

A Seat sign-in feature is planned for the future. When it arrives, sign-in will be handled house-side by The War Cove, and this page will be updated before it goes live.
